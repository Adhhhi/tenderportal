 1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW & OBJECTIVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 The NMPA Tender & Procurement Portal is an end-to-end digital e-tendering 
 web application designed to facilitate transparent, secure, and efficient 
 bidding for port infrastructure development, marine engineering projects, 
 IT modernizations, and material procurements.

 Key Objectives:
 - 100% Digital Tender Creation, Bid Submission, and L1 Price Evaluation.
 - Secure Role-Based Access Control (Public User, Vendor, Dept Admin, Super Admin).
 - Automated Audit Trail Logging and Digital Certificate Generation.
 - Multi-lingual Support (English, Kannada, Hindi, Malayalam).
 - Strict Compliance with Major Port Authorities Act 2021.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 2. PORT DEPARTMENTS & PROCUREMENT SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Civil Engineering Department:
    - Capital & Maintenance Dredging of Port Navigation Channels.
    - Deepening & Construction of Commercial Cargo Berths (Berth 17/18).
    - Breakwater Extensions & Marine Structures Maintenance.

 2. Mechanical Engineering Department:
    - Procurement & Overhaul of Post-Panamax Electric Quay Cranes (EQC).
    - Supply & Annual Maintenance of Reach Stackers & Tugboats.
    - Installation of High-Capacity Rubber Marine Fender Systems.

 3. Electrical Engineering Department:
    - 2.5 MW Solar Photovoltaic Power Plant Expansion (Phase II).
    - Cold Ironing / High Voltage Shore Power Provision for Ocean Vessels.
    - 33kV Port Substation & Underground Cable Network Modernization.

 4. Information Technology & EDP Division:
    - SmartPort ERP System & OCR Automated Port Gate Operations.
    - AI-Powered CCTV Surveillance (AGS) & Perimeter Video Analytics.
    - Integrated Port Command & Operations Center (IPCOC) Infrastructure.

 5. Marine Department:
    - Navigational Buoy Network & AIS Telemetry Systems.
    - Pollution Control Equipment & Environmental Impact Assessments.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 3. BID EVALUATION & CONTRACT WORKFLOW STAGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Stage 01: Draft Tender Created by Department Officer
 Stage 02: Published for Public Bidding & EMD Verification
 Stage 03: Technical & Financial Proposal Submission by Vendors
 Stage 04: Technical Bid Evaluation & Qualification Clearance
 Stage 05: Financial Quote Opening & L1 Lowest Bid Calculation
 Stage 06: Tender Committee Approval & Contract Awarding
 Stage 07: Digital Award Certificate Generation (ISO Compliant)
 Stage 08: Work Order Execution & Milestone Monitoring
 Stage 09: Project Completion Proof Submission & Quality Audit
 Stage 10: Department Review, Final Settlement & Project Closure

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 4. DOCUMENT INTEGRITY & VERIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 All attached tender specifications, technical proposals, bill of quantities (BOQ),
 and compliance certificates generated by the system are cryptographically 
 signed, ISO-certified, and authenticated by the NMPA Board.
